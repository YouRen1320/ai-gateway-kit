# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good faith while evaluating technical claims using evidence.
- Respect privacy and never post another person's credentials or private data.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and correct mistakes.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project and its users' safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Pressuring maintainers to assist with unauthorized access or provider-control bypasses.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report sensitive conduct concerns through the same private channel described in `SECURITY.md`.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and made without retaliation.
